How it works

1. Preprocess

Lowercase, strip punctuation, tokenize, drop stop-words, Porter-stem.

2. Vectorize

TF-IDF places the article in a Vector Space Model.

3. Classify

Logistic Regression outputs the probability of real vs fake.

4. Explain

The most influential terms are shown so the result is not a black box.

The model detects writing patterns, not facts. Always verify important news with trusted sources.
